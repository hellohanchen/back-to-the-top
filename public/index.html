Methodology

Every value is for the selected day, fixed EST (UTC−5), 05:00–05:00 UTC. Charts show individual daily observations; zooming never changes the report into a period total.

USD secondary-market sales use DapperUtilityCoin. Moments are separated into OffersV2 offers and NFTStorefrontV2 listings; Packs show NFTStorefrontV2 listings. Legacy-market sales and primary drops are outside these displayed sales channels. Zero-sale price statistics are shown as —.

Users are unique on-chain addresses, not people. Activity includes observed Top Shot interactions, including trading, listings and cancellations, offers, transfers and locking. Known system accounts are excluded. See the dataset activity coverage metadata for exact tracked contracts, events and unresolved attribution.
