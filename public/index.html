Behind the numbers

Time & coverage

Days run from 05:00 UTC to 05:00 UTC, fixed EST (UTC−5), with no daylight-saving adjustment. Coverage begins October 1, 2026. Only completed days with all required requests successful are published. Pending days are gaps, never zeroes.

Sales & currency

Secondary-market completed sales from NFTStorefrontV2, OffersV2 and TopShotMarketV3. Only the exact TopShot.NFT and PackNFT.NFT types are included. Cancelled listings and offers are excluded. USD figures use DapperUtilityCoin payment amounts; non-USD sales are excluded. Primary pack drops are not secondary-market sales. Events are deduplicated by transaction ID and event index.

Addresses, not people

Activity includes deposits, withdrawals, listings, offers and unambiguous lock transaction signers. Buying and selling addresses are matched to the same NFT inside each sale transaction. All assets uses the deduplicated union across Moments and packs for active, buying and selling addresses. Four known Dapper system accounts are excluded. One person may own multiple addresses.

Locks & quality

Locks and unlocks count events, not unique Moments or current locked supply. A single transaction authorizer is counted as a signer, not asserted to be the owner. Multi-authorizer transactions remain unattributed. See the downloadable JSON for unresolved address matches and currency exclusions.
